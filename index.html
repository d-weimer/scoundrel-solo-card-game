How to Play

  • Goal: Clear the deck and survive with at least 1 HP.
  • Combat: Fight Monsters (Clubs/Spades) by subtracting your Shield value from the monster's value. Any leftover damage hits your HP.
  • Shields: Diamonds grant a shield. You can only use a shield against a monster weaker than the last one you fought with it, or it breaks.
  • Potions: Hearts heal you.
  • Room Rules: You must play 3 cards to move to the next room. You can only use one Potion and one Shield per room; others are discarded.
  • Fleeing: You can flee a room if you haven't played any cards yet, but you cannot flee twice in a row.
